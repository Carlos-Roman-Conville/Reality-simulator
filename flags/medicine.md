## medicine (2529)

- UNSUPPORTED: medicine exposure dose-response, MEDICINE.md section 1 (daily onset chance `dose x hygiene_mult x (1 - immunity)`, per-category rates `bites/day x p_vector`, `contacts x p_transmit`): no library row carries a dose-response or a transmission probability. `expose(disease, dose)` takes `dose` as the host's chance in [0, 1] that this exposure takes; the engine multiplies it by the host's `hygiene` and draws once. Vector, louse, flea, droplet and sexual categories, seasons and places are the host's (environment, population).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine hygiene multipliers (x2 at 7 days unswabbed, x5 lousy, x3 foul water, x4 wet-season port, ...), MEDICINE.md section 6. The library's `exposure` table holds the air conditions, not the hygiene table; those multipliers live only in the game doc. `hygiene` is an input (default 1, no effect). The `exposure` table is implemented as `airConditions` (its own thresholds, read from the text).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine distribution inside a row's range. Incubation, course and wound-healing days are drawn uniformly over the integer days of the row's range; the case's death risk is the point `riskAt` (default the middle) of the row's range. A fatal case's crisis falls on a uniformly drawn course day (the game doc's "risk divided over the course days", made exact so the total is the row's risk). The tables give ranges, not distributions.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine death timing where a row gives a risk but no course (malaria_falciparum: "irregular fever; cerebral malaria within days"): the fatal draw is made and `timingUnsupported` is set on the case; no crisis is placed. Consumption's incubation ("months to years") is null; `expose` refuses it unless the host gives `incubationDays`. Its risk window is the text's own "within 5 years".
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine non-numeric death risks ("high over months" scurvy and beriberi, "moderate over years" pellagra, "near certain" gangrene, "very low", "low", "about a third ... over decades" syphilis): null; no crisis is emitted for them. Yellow fever's toxic phase (15% of cases, after a remission) has no timing; its crisis day is a course day. Yellow fever uses the 5 to 10% "of all cases" clause; other rows use their first clause (bacillary dysentery 5 to 15%, not the amoebic 1 to 5%).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine treated death risk and course length. The treatments table says only that a treatment worked. A "worked" treatment named by the disease row's "What helped, really" (bark on the malarias, citrus on scurvy, purges on worms, amputation on gangrene) cancels the pending untreated-death draw and nothing else; the residual risk with treatment is not in the library. Citrus ends scurvy in the disease row's own "recovery in 1 to 2 weeks"; amputation ends gangrene at once (the column: "the only cure"). Rows that say partly, sometimes, "if done early" or "no" (washing, extraction, splints, opium, trepanning, bleeding, purging fevers, mercury, rum, sweating) change no outcome; the harms they name (worsened blood loss, dehydration, poisoning, "at a cost" of cautery) have no size and are not applied. Amputation's own death risk ("over half died of the operation") is a bound, not a number: not applied. Opium's pain relief belongs to neurology (the pain-gating circuit).
- UNSUPPORTED (fix 8, 2550): hygiene multipliers and treated death risk re-searched in data/library/medicine.json (diseases, treatments, exposure tables and every source line: Woodall, Moyle, Burnard, Hektoen, Cardiff sea-surgeons, MED-* keys). No hygiene multiplier appears in any row or source line, and no row gives a death risk with treatment (the only figure near it, amputation "over half died of the operation", is a bound). Both stay unset; `hygiene` stays the host's input, default 1. A hygiene note was added to the treatments table in medicine.json so the library says so.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine immunity strength. "partial" (falciparum, "mostly immune" typhus) has no protection fraction: the state is kept and fades after "a year" (365 days, by definition) but reduces no onset chance. Malaria vivax relapses, Brill-Zinsser recurrence and the seasoning (a state with "as high as a third", confidence low, and no definition of how many bouts) are not implemented. Only lifelong immunity blocks exposure.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine bleeding rate. The wounds table gives a class label, not mL/min (see circulation above). The host supplies `rateMlMin` and `clotting` together, or the wound does not bleed; the class is a label (drawn across the row's range by `severity`; a tourniquet turns amputation's III into the row's "with tourniquet II"). Burns carry "fluid loss like class II per 15% of body burned" as `burnFluidClassIIUnits` (burned % / 15), not as millilitres. Binding stops the loss but nothing stops it by itself (no clotting law in the library).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine sepsis, tetanus and gangrene from a wound (MEDICINE.md section 3: 5% a day, x0.3 drained, 30% a day death): not in the library. A wound's infection is one roll at the row's chance (the open-wound figure when `open`), pauses its healing clock, and stays until the host clears it; tetanus and gangrene are exposures the host supplies. Washing's "partly" does not lower the chance.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine the consequence of "healed wounds reopen" (scurvy x3 row) and of the working-part "reopen chance 10%/day": the chance is read from the row; a reopened wound's healing progress restarts at 0 (the only reading of "reopen"); scurvy's reopening of healed wounds has no rate and is not applied (anatomy's collagen integrity carries the vitamin C effect). Healing multipliers multiply when several apply. Surgeon skill 50 and "using the part" are the host's (`dressedDaily`, `workingPart`, `resting`); vitamin C, energy balance and sleep debt are read from the world when present, protein only if the host passes it.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine pain beyond the band. The pain table has cause examples and no effects or decay; the level is the worst open wound's band (the midpoint of its range, a stand-in for a value inside the band), 0 with none; no decay "with healing". Kinds with no cause example (thrust, head blow) have no pain source unless the host names a `painCause`. Shock (pain 8 and class II) needs the game's Nerve check and is not computed.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine the air conditions' stages beyond the thresholds the text gives: smoke "with heat and CO" qualifiers, H2S death in 30 to 60 minutes, CO tier timings, hypoxia death "within minutes", delayed brain injury. Each condition is a stage number from the pattern; only hypoxia under its death threshold and respiratory failure open a crisis.

- NOTE: medicine crisis records are `{kind: "crisis", cause, day, risk}` with causes `disease:<id>`, `treatment:<id>:<disease>` and `exposure:<id>`; `resolveCrisis(survived | died)` is the host's. A case with an open crisis waits (its course does not advance) until resolved. Each exposure draws five random numbers and each wound three, whether or not they matter, so a treatment that changes nothing never shifts another case's draws (inoculation and citrus on scurvy draw one number each when they act).
