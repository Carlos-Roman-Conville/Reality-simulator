# FLAGS: what the docs ask for and the library cannot support

One line per gap: `UNSUPPORTED: <module> <what> <which doc asked for it>`. The path stays disabled or `null` until the
library (or a cited source) carries the number. Nothing here is patched with an invented constant.

## physiology (2520)

- UNSUPPORTED: physiology cooling term of the core drift rule (wind, shade, rest, drinking): `coreTemp += over x drift - cooling(...)`, PHYSIOLOGY.md section 4. No library row has a cooling rate, so `core_c` only rises above the ceiling and returns to the resting core at the overnight tick when sheltered (recovery table).
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology shade bonus on the WBGT ceiling (`over = WBGT - ceiling - acclim_bonus - shade_bonus`), PHYSIOLOGY.md section 4. No shade value in `wbgt_limits`; the acclimatisation bonus is read from the table's two ceiling columns instead.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology cold side of the core (the "loss below 20 C felt" drift, shivering cost, wet-night loss), PHYSIOLOGY.md sections 4 and 5. `core_temp` has the bands but no rate; `cold_water` has times, not a rate. `core_c` has no falling path.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology urine (1.5 L) and breath (0.4 L) water loss, PHYSIOLOGY.md section 5 [model, proposed]. Not in `hydration`; only sweat and drinking move the deficit. Food water arrives through `ingest` when the nutrition module supplies it.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology seawater counted as negative water (PHY-SEAWATER), PHYSIOLOGY.md section 5: no library row; `ingest` takes the water it is given.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology skin-temperature shift of the hydration thresholds ("about 1% more per C above 27 C"), PHYSIOLOGY.md section 5: the source text is a rule of thumb, not a table row; `hydrationRow` takes only the table's own heat variant (x0.75 in heat).
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology sleep quality weights other than split (watch) sleep: deck in rain 0.6, in pain 0.7, drunk 0.8, storm 0.7, PHYSIOLOGY.md section 3. Only the split value (0.85) is in the recovery table; the caller passes `sleepQuality` per schedule window and supplies the others.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology the upper cap on sleep debt (60 h), PHYSIOLOGY.md section 3: not in the library. Debt is floored at 0 and not capped; the table's last band ("over 40") already holds its multiplier.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology what counts as a "day of work in heat" for acclimatisation (10 to 14 days to gain, about 25 away to lose, `acclimatisation` table): the library gives the days, not the dose. The module counts a day as heat work when any awake step has WBGT above the work class's unacclimatised ceiling (a table column), gains 1/14 per such day (the range's upper end, full adaptation) and loses 1/25 per other day. A model choice, to be replaced when a source gives the dose.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology heat acclimatisation's other effects (sweat starts sooner, less salt per litre), sea-legs habituation, cold acclimatisation: the `acclimatisation` rows give no numbers for them (sea legs needs the seaway from the environment module).
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology the endurance pool, glycogen factor and Banister fitness and fatigue (`banister` table, `recovery` rows on the endurance pool and Banister decay), PHYSIOLOGY.md sections 2 and 6: they need glycogen (nutrition, 2521) and a baseline performance the library does not carry. Not implemented here.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology a thermoregulating tissue temperature. `body.temperatureC` is the lumped tissue heat balance (metabolic heat minus work, minus h A dT to the air, minus sweat evaporation), with no vasomotor control or clothing insulation (the doc's "upgrade if the simulator finds the shadow too coarse"). `core_c` is the library's drift state; the two are separate outputs.
- UNSUPPORTED: physiology the sweat-rate coefficients (0.3, 0.25, 0.1 L/h; clamp 0.3 to 2.5), the 0.2 factor and 37.0 C gate that cut sweat while the tissue is cool, skin area 1.9 m2 and h 10 W/(m2 K), PHYSIOLOGY.md section 4 [model, proposed] and the still's ENG-HT assumptions. No library row holds them. They moved unchanged from `process.ts` `bodyProcess` so the still keeps its numbers; sweat-at-maximum above 38.5 C core is the `core_temp` row's own text.
- NOTE: the day boundary (`dayStartH`, default 12: the nautical day) and `sheltered` (default 1) and `acclimatisation` (default 1: the table's ceilings are the acclimatised ones) are inputs with defaults, not library numbers.

## nutrition (2521)

- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition deficiency stages 2 and 3, NUTRITION.md depletion table (Stage 1/2/3 face columns). The library's `depletion` table has one level per nutrient ("Signs below"), so stage 1 is that level (vitamin C: 300 mg, day 53, the docs' scurvy day) and stages 2 and 3 are emitted only where the caller passes their pool levels (`NutritionOptions.stages`; the game adapter's rows carry them). The docs' stage-1 vitamin C level of 500 mg (day 36) is a game column, not used.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition pools for nutrients whose reserve the depletion table gives no number for (riboflavin "small", vitamin K "small", essential fatty acids "fat stores", potassium, calcium, zinc, B6, E, D and the rest of `nutrients`): they are not pooled, no stage is emitted; their absorbed intake is reported as `intake_<id>` for the last closed day.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition recovery time where the table gives words, not a number ("days to feel better; 2 to 4 weeks to heal" for vitamin C, "weeks", "months", "days", "hours"): the stage falls the day the pool is back over its level. Numbers it gives (thiamin 1 week, niacin 2 weeks) hold the stage for that long.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition lean mass and protein catabolism ("in a deficit over 1,000 kcal/day, development decays", "muscle itself (catabolised in a deficit)"), body_composition `lean-mass`: needs the MUSCULAR development sum (muscular module) and gives no rate. A deficit past the fat floor is reported as `energy_unplaced_kcal` (negative) and `starvation_limit`, and no lean tissue is burned.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition starting fat mass. The library gives no body-fat fraction, so `fat_kg` is an input; without it the energy balance is reported as `energy_unplaced_kcal` and no fat is stored. Glycogen starts at the table's full reserve (400 g) unless `glycogen_g` is given.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition the starvation stage clock (`starvation-stages`: ketosis days 2 to 7 at work x0.8, wasting weeks 2 to 6 at x0.5): the table does not say what counts as a day of starvation. Only its two death limits are used: fat at its floor with unmet energy, or mass under 60% of the start mass.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition fullness, hunger, appetite and the eating rule (`body_composition`, proposed): not part of this module; no pool depends on them.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition protein quality (`absorption` protein-quality row) and the water and salt lost in a flux ("2 to 4 L"): the table gives a range or needs a plant/animal class and a single-source test per food; protein is taken at x1.0 (x0.5 in a flux), water and salts at x1.0 with no flux loss.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition food classes. The library has no meat, fish, grain, pulse or green flag on a food row, so haem iron (the absorption table's "meat, fish") is chosen by food id in `HAEM_FOODS` (salt beef and pork, salt fish, herring, fresh fish, turtle, boucan, tasajo, coast provisions); maize niacin by an id containing "maize". Wholegrain, tea, cooked, raw fish, nixtamalised and grain-and-pease are meal options the caller sets (tea is also read from a `tea-brewed` item), not properties of a row. Mixed dishes (salmagundi) count as non-haem.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition fat-soluble vitamins in a flux use the table's x0.3 instead of the general x0.5 (the specific rule replaces the general one); the table does not say whether they multiply.
- UNSUPPORTED: nutrition vitamin D, iodine and the remaining `nutrients` rows (no food column, no pool).
- NOTE: nutrition niacin is taken as niacin equivalents (the nutrients unit "mg NE") for the food column "Niacin mg", and folate as the food column "Folate mcg" against the pool in mg; units are converted by definition, not by a source.
- NOTE: nutrition `male` defaults to man (the table's first column) when absent; the day boundary (`dayStartH` 12) matches physiology. Stored fat and glycogen change the body component's mass by the fat only (glycogen water and the oxidised mass of the food are not modelled).
- NOTE: nutrition pools are ledger substances `nutrient:<id>` (empty formula, molarMass 1 as a placeholder that multiplies zero atoms) so `World.conserve` can hold each nutrient's mass; they carry no chemistry. Cumulative energy balance since the body began (marks start at 0) is checked each day as `energy:stored` against glycogen and fat plus the unplaced energy.
- NOTE: physiology now also keeps `props.sweat_kg`, the running total of traced sweat, which the sodium use reads ("1 g per litre of sweat").
