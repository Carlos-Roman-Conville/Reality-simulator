# FLAGS: what the docs ask for and the library cannot support

## circulation (2526)

- UNSUPPORTED: circulation numeric baroreflex, renal-volume and erythropoiesis time constants, CIRCULATION.md homeostasis. The library says only seconds, hours to days, days to weeks. `regulationLoops()` preserves targets, clocks and failure conditions with `tauSeconds: null`. Automatic baroreflex and cell production are disabled. Volume recovery requires an explicit host experimental tau, working kidneys and a finite `circulation_fluid_ml` supply; the test's one-day tau is a named experiment, not a measured library value. Oral absorption and nutritional/marrow coupling remain disabled.
- UNSUPPORTED: circulation cardiac output versus blood-volume deficit and maximal-output fitness/damage mapping, CIRCULATION.md CIR-DO2. Flow remains null unless the host supplies actual flow or resistance (CO = MAP/resistance); maximal output must be supplied. `aerobic_today` is DO2 at that supplied maximum. Class III's drop in the test uses an explicit fixed-resistance fixture. No invented volume-to-flow slope or fitness constant is installed.
- UNSUPPORTED: circulation post-resuscitation pressure/perfusion kinetics, CIRCULATION.md bleeding/homeostasis. Class labels use cumulative blood loss as specified. Acute class pressure/perfusion effects are unavailable after replacement fluid enters, pending a recovery mapping. Class IV supplies only MAP under 50, so exact pressure is null; unspecified organs are null. Rounded mL examples differ from percentages of 4,900 mL; percentage bands govern, with III beginning at 30% and IV strictly above 40%. A 1,500 mL loss is III either way.
- UNSUPPORTED: circulation wound rates, CIRCULATION.md bleeding. There are no numeric wound mL/min rates in the table. The host supplies rate, clotting and binding; the library's rate x minutes x (2 - clotting) is used. Blood volume and haemoglobin have separate traced ledgers checked every step; volume is never represented as fictitious mass. Replacement fluid is an explicit volume supply, not silently removed from physiology's water or counted twice as body mass.
- UNSUPPORTED: circulation event/death chances are UNMEASURED placeholders, CIRCULATION.md events/bleeding. `hazard` exposes the table coefficient in its native per-hour/day/event unit with `unmeasured: true`, not a calibrated continuous hazard. It never rolls, damages or kills. Multiple arrhythmia causes have no combining rule; hypoxia/fever, non-immobility thrombosis and non-index stroke have unsized rates (null). Sustained low function and very high pressure require explicit host predicates because thresholds/durations are incomplete. Atherosclerosis accumulation remains null because age interpolation and risk-index scales are unspecified; its original rule is exposed.
- UNSUPPORTED: circulation quantitative acid-base, curve shifts and hydrogen-sulphide cellular inhibition, CIRCULATION.md blood_gas. Outputs stay null. Saturation and COHb come from atmosphere; no normal-air fallback hides absent gas inputs. DO2 uses bound oxygen only as requested (CIR-DO2: CO x 1.34 x Hb x SaO2 x 10), corrected by (1 - COHb). Dissolved oxygen is omitted. Other quantity normals and delivery failure treatments are available by library id as source rules, not unimplemented live physiology.

One line per gap: `UNSUPPORTED: <module> <what> <which doc asked for it>`. The path stays disabled or `null` until the
library (or a cited source) carries the number. Nothing here is patched with an invented constant.

- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere grid transport rates. `gasdynamics.grid` gives the rules (advection by the wind, diffusion by "D x step / cell size squared", buoyancy, deposition) as `deferred` text with no D, no rise or settle rate and no wind field. - UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere deposition and sinks other than ventilation (`sources_sinks` "surfaces, water, rain": SO2 and H2S lost to wet surfaces, particles settle "half in an hour in still air", rain washes smoke; `grid.deposition`). The rates are "placeholders" with no number; particles and smoke are not registry substances. Not implemented.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere source rates the rows do not give: a lamp's CO "if it gutters", a brazier's O2 and CO2 (only "CO 0.5 L/min in a shut cabin"; charcoal burns by `combustionProcess`), a fire's own O2 (derived from the carbon: O2 = CO2 + CO / 2) and smoke ("1 g/min", no smoke substance), black powder (330 cm3 of gas per gram, but no split among CO2, N2, CO, H2S, H2, CH4), rot's CH4 and CO2, plants ("negligible"), natural vents ("by the place"), a person's water vapour in breath (physiology flags its breath water loss). Only the species with a rate are emitted.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere livestock "as a person x body-mass ratio": the body source is VO2 = 3.5 x MET x mass (`physiology.ventilation`) times the respiratory quotient 0.8 (`gasdynamics.box_model`), which needs no reference mass; it reproduces the person row (0.25 O2, 0.2 CO2 at 70 kg and 1 MET) to 3% and the goat row (0.1 at 28 kg).
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere the A-a gradient's rise with lung damage ("5 to 10 mmHg in health, more with damage", `anatomy.respiratory`), exchange capacity (diffusing capacity, mL O2 per min per mmHg), airway resistance, dead space, tissue integrity and the transfer `capacity x gradient`: no number for how any of them changes the gradient. `RespiratoryProfile.aaGradientMmHg` is the caller's lever; the others are not used. Gills and water breathing (`medium` water or other) give null outputs and no exchange.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere shifts of the saturation curve by pH, temperature and CO2, and the left shift by COHb (`circulation.blood_gas`, "shifted by..."): the library has no coefficients. SaO2 is the plain Severinghaus form. The oxygen content with CO (`CaO2`) is the circulation module's.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere ventilation when the CO2 drive fails ("over 70 mmHg the drive fails and sleepiness wins", `physiology.ventilation`): no number for the fall. The drive is held at its 70 mmHg value (and the profile maximum) above it.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere the uptake fraction of a gas in the inhaled dose (`inhaledDoseLitres`; CHEMISTRY): the library names it without a value, so the caller passes it. Exposure integration is ppm-minutes and the peak per gas; COHb is the only dose model.
- UNSUPPORTED: atmosphere mixing that "restores" the x1.5 low and x0.5 high layers (`gasdynamics.box_model`): no rate, so `layeredPpm` takes `mixed` as the caller's flag; CO (0.97 x air) and the other gases the row does not name read the mean. The grid hands a cloud to the box model when every species is within 10% of its mean: that 10% is the box-model row's tolerance for return to baseline, reused for uniformity.
- NOTE: atmosphere the pirate dashboard's respiratory calculator coefficients that no table carries are used as cited and are illustrative there: arterial PO2 = alveolar PO2 less 7 mmHg (the library says 5 to 10), PaCO2 = 40 + FiCO2 (P - 47) x 0.7 for retained inspired CO2, the hypoxic drive (when PAO2 is under 60 and CO2 under 2%, PaCO2 falls by 0.5 per mmHg short, at most 15, to a floor of 25; ventilation rises by drop / 15), and the COHb equilibrium cap of 0.8. They are `CALCULATOR` in `src/atmosphere.ts`. The maximal ventilation is the upper end (150 L/min) of the human row "100 to 150".
- NOTE: atmosphere the altitude table's resting saturation at 2,256, 3,000 and 5,000 m (0.90, 0.88, 0.75) is a typical reading that includes some acclimatisation (over-breathing), which the formula only models by the calculator's hypoxic drive: it gives 0.876, 0.845 and 0.701 (0.971, 0.961 and 0.947 at 0, 500 and 1,000 m against 0.97, 0.96 and 0.95). The test compares 0 to 1,000 m to 0.01 and 2,256 m to 0.03; the pressure, inspired and alveolar PO2 columns agree to under 1 mmHg in every row.
- NOTE: atmosphere a flow in litres per minute is litres of gas at the room's own temperature and pressure (`gasdynamics.units`, moles = p V / (R T)); physiology's oxygen consumption is usually quoted at standard conditions, about 9% more moles per litre at 25 C. The sources are placeholders to that precision.
- NOTE: atmosphere the outside air's water, 0.012 kg per kg of dry air (`OUTSIDE_WATER_KG_PER_KG`), is the still's value, moved with the room box from `process.ts` unchanged; the baseline table gives only "0 to 4% by humidity". Nitrogen fills the balance of the oxygen row (the still's 0.7905), so the argon row is folded into it.
- NOTE: atmosphere breathing moves O2 from the room to the body and CO2 from the body to the room as traced exchanges (`mass:o2`, `mass:co2`, process `breathing`); nutrition's pools do not yet book the carbon and oxygen of the oxidised food, so a per-element ledger that includes the body would not close. `physiologyProcess` integrates exposure through `accumulateExposure` (ppm-minutes and the peak per gas) and `breathingProcess` does not repeat it.

## environment (2523)

Liljegren's model was not implemented (needs radiation, wind and globe terms the host does not supply).
- UNSUPPORTED: environment wind and sun as terms of WBGT, ENVIRONMENT.md exposure table ("shade and wind lower it"). - UNSUPPORTED: environment mosquito and foul-water exposure by terrain (`vector_exposure`), ENVIRONMENT.md section 5 and MEDICINE.md. The game's table has the multipliers, but the library's `environment.exposure` has no such columns (Shade, Wind felt, WBGT vs open deck, Wet only), so `vector_exposure` is `null`. - UNSUPPORTED: environment shade and wind words without a number: `sun_exposure` is 1 for "none", 0 for "full" (and 0 at night) and `null` for "some", "palms", "buildings"; the felt wind is the host's wind x1 for "full", x0 for "none" and `null` for "little", "more", "trades", "some", "gale" (so `felt_c` is `null` in the cold on those rows). The library gives the words, not the shares.
- UNSUPPORTED: environment wet cold (soaked, spray, damp, rain in the cold) beyond the row's word: no library row turns wetness into a felt temperature or a loss rate (see also the physiology cold side). `physiology.cold_water` is read as the band, its times in minutes (`immersion_*_min_lo/_hi`), not as a cooling rate.
- UNSUPPORTED: environment the diurnal curve's extreme hours. The library gives none (the host's climate table owns the month's maximum, minimum and the hours they fall); `diurnalC` takes them as arguments. The half-cosine between extremes is a standard interpolation, not retrieved from a single citable page here.
- NOTE: environment day length is a day centred on 12:00 solar time (sunrise 12 - daylight/2); the month's daylight hours and sunburn minutes (the `environment.sun` rows) are the host's and are not in the library file.
- NOTE: environment wind chill is the North American index (Osczevski and Bluestein 2005), defined for air at or under 10 C and wind at or over 4.8 km/h; outside that `windChillC` is `null` and `felt_c` is the air. The coefficients are the published ones, not library rows. The test compares -10 C at 20 km/h to the chart's -18.
- NOTE: environment moves no mass or energy (it reads conditions and writes outputs), so there is nothing to trace or conserve; `props.wbgt_c` on the place is the value physiology reads.

## neurology (2524)

- UNSUPPORTED: neurology every EC50 (`receptor` clock 1: `occupancy = C / (C + EC50)`), NEUROLOGY.md section 3: "EC50 per substance and system is the placeholder the simulator tunes", no number in `pk` or `receptor`. `NeuroParams.ec50` (per substance key, in the substance's concentration unit) is the caller's; without it the substance has a concentration (`conc_<key>`, Widmark for alcohol) and no acute effect (`occupancy_<key>` is `null`). The "standard dose is what a potency of 1.0 means" sentence gives no way to fix an EC50 without a reference body and a saturation level.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the tolerance rate k_tol (`receptor` clock 2: `sensitivity -= k_tol x mean occupancy over the day, floor 0.3`). The row gives the floor (0.3), the recovery constants (the `systems` days) and timescales in words ("alcohol 2 to 3 weeks of daily drinking for marked tolerance"), not the rate. `NeuroParams.kTol` is `null` by default: no tolerance builds, so no dependence and no withdrawal either. The tests pass their own k_tol and check the formula, not an agreement with a measured tolerance.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the rebound peak of clock 4 (`rebound_s(t)` "rising from the drug's last elimination to a peak then fading on the recovery constant"). Only the opioids have a peak in the library ("peak 1 to 3 days", taken at its middle, 48 h); alcohol, caffeine and nicotine have onsets and complications in hours but no peak. `NeuroParams.reboundPeakH` supplies it per substance; the rise is linear from the last dose and the fade is exponential on the system's recovery constant (the shape is a model choice; the library gives only the words). "The drug's last elimination" is read as the last dose: a first-order drug never finishes eliminating.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the withdrawal swing's size. `withdrawal_s = dependence_s x (1 - occupancy) x rebound_s(t)` is dimensionless; the module scales it by the drug's own potency on the system and reverses its sign ("tone swings opposite to the drug"), so a fully adapted system rebounds by what the drug moved it. Every system the substance hits swings, not only those the withdrawal text names (nicotine's noradrenaline hit also rebounds). `occupancy` in the factor is the system's own, the sum over substances held to 1.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology exposure days and the dependence bounds. "Exposure days" is not defined; the module counts closed days on which the system had a mean occupancy above zero, resets the count when the sensitivity is back to 1 after idle days, and holds dependence to 0..1 (the formula `clamp(1 - sensitivity, 0, 1) x exposure days / 14` has no upper bound). Because the tolerance floor caps `1 - sensitivity` at 0.7, dependence over the delirium line (0.7) takes more than 14 exposure days at the floor; the library does not say whether that is intended.
- NOTE: neurology a first-order tail never ends, so an amount under 1e-12 kg (the registry's own floor for a content) is zero: a substance is "cleared" and a day without exposure is possible. This is a numerical floor, not a model constant.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the later adenosine rise of alcohol ("adenosine +0.1 later (the rebound insomnia)"): no delay is given, so that hit is off (`delayed` on the hit). Laudanum's "gut motility down" and quinine's cinchonism (hearing, vision, nausea over 2 g a day) name no system and are not modelled.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology habit strength per substance (`reward-and-habit`: "learns what delivered the signal"), craving and the reward from relief during withdrawal (`interactions`): the library gives no learning rule. The reward output is the weighted read of dopamine and opioid tone only.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the inverted-U terms: gaba in `motor-control` ("both under 0.7 and over 1.3 impair") and noradrenaline in `attention` (+0.3 "inverted U"). The library gives the band for gaba but no penalty size, and no band for noradrenaline; the terms are off (`disabledTerms`), so motor-control reads acetylcholine and dopamine only and a drunk man's coordination does not yet fall. `breathing-drive` does not use the linear read: it is the product form of the `interactions` row, which carries the alcohol term.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology sleep quality and the sleep circuit's second output ("sleep propensity and sleep quality"): one weighted read is emitted, as propensity; no quality rule.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the size of several baseline drivers: sleep debt over 24 h (glutamate control down, noradrenaline erratic), heat strain's serotonin ("disturbed"), fever ("all systems noisy"), deficiency (thiamin, niacin, B12, iron: "slow loss", "months on repletion"), genetics (no range). They are listed in `BaselineResult.unsized` when active and move nothing; deficiency and genetics are not read. Starvation stage 2, pain, hunger and fear are inputs from the host (`starvation_stage`, `pain`, `hunger`, `fear_event`): nutrition and medicine do not yet output them.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the decay times of drivers whose `Decays` column is a word: "hours" (exercise: the opioid +0.2 holds the 2 h the row gives), "a day" (praise and the fear day term hold until the day's books close), "with the pain", "with a meal", "with cooling" (the driver follows its state), "weeks" (isolation). Isolation's "-0.1 a day while it lasts" is read as a level of -0.1 while it lasts, not a daily accumulation. The fear day term (+0.1) adds to the event term (+0.5) during the event.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology adenosine's clearance by sleep ("reset by sleep (deep sleep clears it)"): no rate, so any sleeping step sets the hours awake to 0 (`props.asleep`, or physiology's effective sleep hours growing). Short dozes therefore reset it; the sleep-debt table stays the physiology output.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology ageing's acetylcholine rate ("-3%"): taken per decade as for dopamine, and read as a continuous scale on the tone after 40.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the dose route and absorption beyond one half-time: "slower with food" has no number (only the availability, 66 to 71%, used at its middle, 68.5%), and a chewed quid or a skin unction is the row's half-time as given. Tobacco's standard dose is "1 to 2 mg": the range is kept (`standardDose`) and the caller passes the amount. Mercury and lead have no volume of distribution and no effect size: they are traced body burdens with a half-life (`burden_<key>`), and "tone shifts grow with burden and fade over months" has no size. Quinine and the tagged row ("as its row") have no hits.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the liver's function. Every clearance is multiplied by `props.liver_function` (default 1); the anatomy module that would supply it is not built.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the alcohol withdrawal stage windows. "Onset 6 to 24 h", "fits 24 to 48 h" and "delirium 48 to 72 h if dependence over 0.7" are taken at their lower ends from the last dose; the end of each window, and any dependence threshold below the delirium one, are not in the library, so any dependence above 0 reports onset and seizure by the clock. `deliriumDeathRollPerDay` (10%) is returned by `clockRules`, not rolled.
- UNSUPPORTED: neurology the library has no `outputs` table (NEUROLOGY.md has one, with a "Replaces" column). The circuit rows' `Goes to` text is kept on `Circuit.goesTo`; the module emits outputs by circuit id and writes to no meter.
- NOTE: neurology the circuit read is `1 + sum of weight x (tone - 1)` (a circuit at rest reads 1 whatever its weights sum to), shown in the row's own range: the read itself for the 0 to 2 and x0.5 to x1.5 outputs, `read - 1` for mood bias (-1 to +1) and pain relief (negative in withdrawal, as the row says), and the read held to 0..1 for coordination, encoding and respiratory drive. The tone range 0 to 2 and the sensitivity range come from the `systems` source text.
- NOTE: neurology memory encoding is 0 while alcohol is over 0.08 g/dL (the `memory-and-learning` row: "blocks encoding"); the row does not say it falls gradually. The fever row's attention -0.3 is applied to the attention read, the one circuit it names, not to a system.
- NOTE: neurology alcohol's concentration is the Widmark form `A / (r W)` in g/dL (A in grams, W in kg, r from the Vd column, divided by 10), against the row's own zero-order 0.015 g/dL per hour; the test integrates the closed form `D (1 - 2^(-t/ta)) - beta t` and gets the peak (about 83 min, 0.042 g/dL for 35 g in 70 kg) and the clearing time to within two minutes.
- NOTE: neurology drug mass is a ledger substance `drug:<key>` (mass only), moved intake -> body (`dose`), body -> elimination (the kinetics) and intake -> unabsorbed (the meal share), so `World.conserve("drug:alcohol", ["body"], 1e-12)` holds it every step; the tolerance, dependence and rebound clocks carry no mass.

## anatomy (2527)

- NOTE: `src/anatomy.ts` builds registry organ components and typed blood supply/return edges, tissue components (including muscle), and explicit graph adequacy inputs. Organ ids are the library's bare ids (`heart`, `kidneys`, etc.); tissues retain their full library ids. Shell mass is zero because these are views into the host body's already-counted mass; unknown organ masses are `null`, not zero-size organs. Tissue structural wiring follows the `Where` column. Blood volume/Hb transfers remain in circulation, gas transfers in atmosphere, and food/water/nutrient transfers in nutrition. `feedAssembly` uses the intestines' absorption-times-function rule.
- NOTE: function = minimum input adequacy times (1 - damage), following `damage_function.function-from-inputs` and respecting the ceiling in `two-numbers`. Inputs are sampled before publication so a graph cycle cannot depend on enumeration order. Call `anatomyProcess.step(world, 0)` after input-change events; daily damage/repair and collagen maintenance occur on accumulated day boundaries. Host insults are per-day increments, repair requires an explicit nutrition gate, and tissue integrity can be supplied by the host. Unconnected blood supply or unknown input returns null. A newly assembled, unchallenged body starts healthy; this is an initial condition, not proof of adequate environmental supply.
- NOTE: crisis floors (brain/heart/lungs 0.2, liver/kidneys 0.1) are cited from `E:/AI Programs/Games/RPG's/pirate-ship/files/pirate-ship/body/ANATOMY.md` section 3a, 2026-10-29. They are not currently library rows. Reaching a floor opens a crisis immediately; the host receives elapsed duration and remaining capacity, never an automatic death. A class IV bleed opens a crisis too. No listed death risk is rolled.
- UNSUPPORTED: anatomy quantitative failure cascades and their clocks, ANATOMY.md organs/cascades and timescales. Kidney potassium/waste accumulation, liver toxins/clotting, arrhythmia-to-output, brain-to-respiratory-drive and organ-specific oxygen/fuel/temperature adequacy conversions have no numeric transfer functions or rates. Active qualitative cascade statements are exposed with `kineticsSeconds: null`; they do not change concentrations or downstream function. `connectOrganInput` accepts independently quantified host adequacies, not a guessed equivalence between organ function and electrolyte adequacy. The 10-minute kinetics layer remains disabled. In particular, class IV gives brain perfusion 0.5 but no kidney perfusion; kidney function is null. The requested full timed class-IV kidney/potassium/heart cascade acceptance case is blocked on those missing rows; the test verifies only the supported first stage and crisis.
- UNSUPPORTED: anatomy organ sizes and horse ventilation/Hb, ANATOMY.md species profiles. No organ masses are tabulated; the horse's larger gut is qualitative. `organMassKg` entries, horse `respiratory` and horse `haemoglobinGDL` stay null. Horse assembly builds; its circulation/breathing factories reject absent parameters instead of adopting human values. Host-supplied Hb enables the shared blood ledger using the profile's 80 mL/kg. Human default Hb is the circulation table's male reference. Resting cardiac output uses the species field; output under impaired heart function needs host flow/resistance. The human respiratory profile delegates to atmosphere, including that module's existing flagged assumptions. Equine digestion/metabolism and muscle layout remain data tasks; `feedAssembly` refuses unsupported species diets.
- UNSUPPORTED: anatomy ischemic damage rate and most tissue maintenance rates, ANATOMY.md damage_function and tissues. The under-0.3/over-an-hour statement names an insult but gives no damage increment, so it does not manufacture damage. Explicit host insults/resistance, the listed daily repair rates, and collagen's vitamin-C-under-300-mg decline of 0.02/day are supported. Other nutrient-to-integrity conversions, thermal organ simulation, and automatic liver/brain feedback into neurology remain disabled. Metadata retains each tissue's maintenance requirements and each organ's dependencies/outputs/cascade.

## medicine (2529)

- UNSUPPORTED: medicine exposure dose-response, MEDICINE.md section 1 (daily onset chance `dose x hygiene_mult x (1 - immunity)`, per-category rates `bites/day x p_vector`, `contacts x p_transmit`): no library row carries a dose-response or a transmission probability. `expose(disease, dose)` takes `dose` as the host's chance in [0, 1] that this exposure takes; the engine multiplies it by the host's `hygiene` and draws once. Vector, louse, flea, droplet and sexual categories, seasons and places are the host's (environment, population).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine hygiene multipliers (x2 at 7 days unswabbed, x5 lousy, x3 foul water, x4 wet-season port, ...), MEDICINE.md section 6. The library's `exposure` table holds the air conditions, not the hygiene table; those multipliers live only in the game doc. `hygiene` is an input (default 1, no effect). The `exposure` table is implemented as `airConditions` (its own thresholds, read from the text).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine distribution inside a row's range. Incubation, course and wound-healing days are drawn uniformly over the integer days of the row's range; the case's death risk is the point `riskAt` (default the middle) of the row's range. A fatal case's crisis falls on a uniformly drawn course day (the game doc's "risk divided over the course days", made exact so the total is the row's risk). The tables give ranges, not distributions.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine death timing where a row gives a risk but no course (malaria_falciparum: "irregular fever; cerebral malaria within days"): the fatal draw is made and `timingUnsupported` is set on the case; no crisis is placed. Consumption's incubation ("months to years") is null; `expose` refuses it unless the host gives `incubationDays`. Its risk window is the text's own "within 5 years".
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine non-numeric death risks ("high over months" scurvy and beriberi, "moderate over years" pellagra, "near certain" gangrene, "very low", "low", "about a third ... over decades" syphilis): null; no crisis is emitted for them. Yellow fever's toxic phase (15% of cases, after a remission) has no timing; its crisis day is a course day. Yellow fever uses the 5 to 10% "of all cases" clause; other rows use their first clause (bacillary dysentery 5 to 15%, not the amoebic 1 to 5%).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine treated death risk and course length. The treatments table says only that a treatment worked. A "worked" treatment named by the disease row's "What helped, really" (bark on the malarias, citrus on scurvy, purges on worms, amputation on gangrene) cancels the pending untreated-death draw and nothing else; the residual risk with treatment is not in the library. Citrus ends scurvy in the disease row's own "recovery in 1 to 2 weeks"; amputation ends gangrene at once (the column: "the only cure"). Rows that say partly, sometimes, "if done early" or "no" (washing, extraction, splints, opium, trepanning, bleeding, purging fevers, mercury, rum, sweating) change no outcome; the harms they name (worsened blood loss, dehydration, poisoning, "at a cost" of cautery) have no size and are not applied. Amputation's own death risk ("over half died of the operation") is a bound, not a number: not applied. Opium's pain relief belongs to neurology (the pain-gating circuit).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine immunity strength. "partial" (falciparum, "mostly immune" typhus) has no protection fraction: the state is kept and fades after "a year" (365 days, by definition) but reduces no onset chance. Malaria vivax relapses, Brill-Zinsser recurrence and the seasoning (a state with "as high as a third", confidence low, and no definition of how many bouts) are not implemented. Only lifelong immunity blocks exposure.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine bleeding rate. The wounds table gives a class label, not mL/min (see circulation above). The host supplies `rateMlMin` and `clotting` together, or the wound does not bleed; the class is a label (drawn across the row's range by `severity`; a tourniquet turns amputation's III into the row's "with tourniquet II"). Burns carry "fluid loss like class II per 15% of body burned" as `burnFluidClassIIUnits` (burned % / 15), not as millilitres. Binding stops the loss but nothing stops it by itself (no clotting law in the library).
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine sepsis, tetanus and gangrene from a wound (MEDICINE.md section 3: 5% a day, x0.3 drained, 30% a day death): not in the library. A wound's infection is one roll at the row's chance (the open-wound figure when `open`), pauses its healing clock, and stays until the host clears it; tetanus and gangrene are exposures the host supplies. Washing's "partly" does not lower the chance.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine the consequence of "healed wounds reopen" (scurvy x3 row) and of the working-part "reopen chance 10%/day": the chance is read from the row; a reopened wound's healing progress restarts at 0 (the only reading of "reopen"); scurvy's reopening of healed wounds has no rate and is not applied (anatomy's collagen integrity carries the vitamin C effect). Healing multipliers multiply when several apply. Surgeon skill 50 and "using the part" are the host's (`dressedDaily`, `workingPart`, `resting`); vitamin C, energy balance and sleep debt are read from the world when present, protein only if the host passes it.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine pain beyond the band. The pain table has cause examples and no effects or decay; the level is the worst open wound's band (the midpoint of its range, a stand-in for a value inside the band), 0 with none; no decay "with healing". Kinds with no cause example (thrust, head blow) have no pain source unless the host names a `painCause`. Shock (pain 8 and class II) needs the game's Nerve check and is not computed.
- UNSUPPORTED: medicine the air conditions' stages beyond the thresholds the text gives: smoke "with heat and CO" qualifiers, H2S death in 30 to 60 minutes, CO tier timings, hypoxia death "within minutes", delayed brain injury. Each condition is a stage number from the pattern; only hypoxia under its death threshold and respiratory failure open a crisis.
- NOTE: medicine crisis records are `{kind: "crisis", cause, day, risk}` with causes `disease:<id>`, `treatment:<id>:<disease>` and `exposure:<id>`; `resolveCrisis(survived | died)` is the host's. A case with an open crisis waits (its course does not advance) until resolved. Each exposure draws five random numbers and each wound three, whether or not they matter, so a treatment that changes nothing never shifts another case's draws (inoculation and citrus on scurvy draw one number each when they act).

- UNSUPPORTED: ecology price and duty consequences of a fished-out ground ("the port's fish price up", "the fisherman duty yields little"): economy and duties are the game's. The boucanier history row is a note, not a rule; hunting uses the same stock model with the host's rates.

- UNSUPPORTED: population adults to elders: the ageing row gives children to adults (1/5475 a day, 15 years) and "adults -> elders at 50" without a rate. The engine uses the cohort's width, 35 years (1/12,775 a day), which the table implies and does not state.
- UNSUPPORTED: population health recovery and epidemic effect on health. The food row lowers health 0.02 a day in famine; the table has no recovery rate and no health cost of an outbreak. - UNSUPPORTED: population epidemic details. Transmission runs for the disease row's course days (point of the range given by `riskAt`, default the middle) and each case's death risk is the row's point risk, spread evenly over the course days (the expected value of medicine's uniformly drawn crisis day). Diseases with no day course or no numeric risk cannot run an epidemic (the first throws; the second adds no deaths). Only a lifelong immunity row (medicine `immunityRule`) moves survivors into `seasonedShare`; partial immunity changes nothing, as in medicine. No treatment, quarantine or mortality-with-age (typhus) acts on cohorts; sick people do not yet lower births or labour beyond the `labour` output. The sick count in `labour` includes those who have since died.
- UNSUPPORTED: population migration out ("fear of fever x epidemic") and the unnamed recruit pool: leavers are host numbers; the transient pool is changed only by host arrivals and departures and is not subject to births, deaths or ageing. `enslaved` and `occupations` cohort fields are not modelled (the first is Rex's open question; the second has no row).
